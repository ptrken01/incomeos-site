Why local-first AI tooling

Local-first AI tools offer critical advantages for privacy, control, and cost efficiency. Unlike cloud-based solutions, these tools process data on your own hardware, eliminating concerns about data leakage or third-party access. They're particularly valuable for organizations handling sensitive information, such as healthcare, finance, or government sectors.

Local deployment also reduces dependency on internet connectivity and avoids ongoing subscription costs. For developers and researchers, local-first tools provide complete control over model training, fine-tuning, and inference processes, enabling experimentation without external constraints.

How to evaluate if a tool is free for commercial use

Commercial viability requires careful license review. While many projects appear open-source, some employ "fair-code" licenses that restrict commercial usage. Notable examples include n8n's fair-code licensing, which can create legal uncertainty for business applications.

Check for explicit permissive licenses (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD) rather than copyleft licenses (GPL) or restrictive terms. Look for clear commercial use clauses in license files and project documentation. Some projects may offer dual licensing options, where commercial use requires separate agreements.
