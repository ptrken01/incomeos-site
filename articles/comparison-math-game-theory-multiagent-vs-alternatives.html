Deep Dive: Game-Theoretic Multi-Agent Strategy Pack

  • Competitor Pricing Modeling: Predicting rival actions based on strategic behavior.
  • Nash Equilibrium Business Strategies: Finding stable outcomes where no agent can benefit unilaterally.
  • Multi-Agent Strategy AI: Enabling agents to make decisions considering other agents' potential moves.
