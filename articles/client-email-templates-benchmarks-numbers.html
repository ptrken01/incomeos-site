The Code of Effective Templates

Here's a runnable template structure that consistently performs:

```python

def generate_client_email_template(client_data, service_type):

return f"""

Subject: {client_data['name']}, {service_type} Opportunity

Hi {client_data['first_name']},

I noticed your recent {client_data['company']} work on {client_data['project']}.

Based on my experience helping {client_data['industry']} businesses like yours, I've seen {percentage}% improvement in {metric} within 90 days.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call to discuss how we might approach this together?

Best,

[Your Name]

"""
```
