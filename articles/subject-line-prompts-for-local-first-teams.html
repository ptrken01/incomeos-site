The Prompt Architecture

Each prompt is structured as a single, executable instruction:

```python

def generate_subject_line(topic, audience_type, urgency_level):

return f"Subject Line: {topic} for {audience_type} - {urgency_level}"

```

This approach enables teams to plug in their content and audience parameters to generate high-conversion subject lines.
