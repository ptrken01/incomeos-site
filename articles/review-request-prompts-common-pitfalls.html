The Core Issues

Most review request templates fail because they're generic or overly complex. They either lack personalization or contain too many variables for consistent execution. The key is finding the sweet spot between automation and human touch.

Here's a working example from our prompt library:

```python

def generate_review_request_template(client_name, service_type, days_since_visit):

templates = [

f"Hi {client_name},\n\nWe hope you enjoyed your {service_type} experience with us! Would you mind sharing your feedback?\n\nThanks,\nThe Team",

f"Hello {client_name},\n\nYour recent {service_type} service was a pleasure to provide. Could you take 2 minutes to leave a review?\n\nBest regards"

]

return templates[0] if days_since_visit > 30 else templates[1]

```

This simple function returns different templates based on timing, ensuring relevance without manual switching.

Common Mistake: Over-Engineering

Many practitioners create 50+ variations of the same prompt. This creates confusion and maintenance overhead. Our library provides exactly 50 carefully selected prompts that cover all major service categories with minimal variation.

Common Mistake: Ignoring Timing

Review requests sent too soon or too late often get ignored. The optimal timing window is 3-7 days post-service, but we've found 21-day follow-ups work well for premium services.

Common Mistake: Generic Language

Generic phrases like "We appreciate your business" don't convert. Our prompts use specific service language and emotional triggers based on industry research.
