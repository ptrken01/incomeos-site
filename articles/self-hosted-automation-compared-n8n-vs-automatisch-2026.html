Self-Hosted Automation Compared: n8n vs Automatisch (Why n8n Costs You, Automatisch Doesn't)

Two open-source workflow automators dominate the self-hosted conversation: n8n and Automatisch. They look similar in a screenshot, but their licenses change what "free" actually means for a business — and that difference is the whole reason to pick one over the other. Here is the honest comparison and a working local deploy.

For the full breakdown with a runnable, hardened docker-compose, see Self-Hosted Automation Compared on Gumroad.

The license trap (read this first)

  • n8n is published under a *fair-code* license, not a standard open-source one. The license forbids using n8n to build a product that competes with n8n, and — more practically — the free self-hosted tier is constrained for commercial use. Teams hit paid-plan walls (SSO, advanced roles, some scaling features) faster than the marketing suggests.
  • Automatisch is MIT. Self-host it freely, including for commercial use, with no seat-based ceiling and no "enterprise" gate in front of core features.

If you are automating your own business, both technically work. If you are a solo founder or a small team watching burn, the license is the entire story.

Capability comparison

| Area | n8n | Automatisch |

|------|-----|-------------|

| License | Fair-code (commercial limits) | MIT (free commercial) |

| Built-in integrations | Very large marketplace | Growing, community-driven |

| Trigger model | Webhook, cron, app events | Webhook, cron, app events |

| Self-host difficulty | Moderate (queues, DB) | Light (single compose) |

| Per-seat cost | Yes, on paid tiers | No |

| Best for | Teams wanting max integrations | Solos/small teams avoiding lock-in |

When to pick which

| You need… | Pick |

|-----------|------|

| MIT, truly free commercial self-host | Automatisch |

| Largest built-in integration marketplace | n8n (watch the license) |

| Avoid per-seat surprises as you grow | Automatisch |
