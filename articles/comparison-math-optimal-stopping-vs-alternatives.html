Deep Dive: Optimal-Stopping Decisions Playbook for Agents

This playbook is built around core mathematical frameworks like the secretary problem and optimal stopping theory. It helps agents make decisions in situations where you must choose the best option from a sequence of candidates or opportunities without the ability to go back.

What it does well:

  • Offers practical methods for sequential decision-making under uncertainty.
  • Includes real-world examples and use cases (e.g., hiring, investment timing).
  • Teaches how to calculate stopping rules using probability distributions.
  • Bridges theory and application, making complex math accessible for AI agents.

What it does NOT do:

  • Does not include predictive models or forecasting tools.
  • Lacks integration with other optimization or machine learning workflows.
  • Not designed for solving scheduling or resource allocation problems.
  • No code or automation templates included—this is a conceptual guide only.

Who it’s for:

This playbook is ideal for AI agents working in recruitment, investment, product launches, or any scenario where timing matters. It's especially helpful when the decision involves a single pass through options with no recall.
