Deep Dive: Recursive Meta-Loop Engineering — Level 2: Mechanism Injection

Mechanism Injection addresses a specific but critical gap in AI agent development: how to ensure that feedback loops actually result in meaningful improvements rather than just more documentation. It builds on the foundation of Level 1 by introducing mechanisms for self-improvement at the system level.

What it does well:

  • Provides a clear framework for injecting improvement mechanisms into existing loops
  • Focuses specifically on turning "wiki notes" into actionable edits
  • Assumes you already have working systems (Level 1) and want to evolve them

What it does NOT do:

  • Doesn't address basic agent architecture or training
  • Doesn't provide pricing strategy frameworks (that's the alternative)
