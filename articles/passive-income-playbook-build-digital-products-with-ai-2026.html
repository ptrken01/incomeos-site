The loop

1. Find paid problems. Look at what people already buy in a niche, not what's trending.

2. Scope a tiny v1. One PDF, one prompt pack, one checklist. Shippable in days.

3. Build with AI, edit like an editor. Generate the draft, then verify every claim and number yourself.

4. List where the buyers are. A marketplace with built-in demand beats a lonely landing page.

Validate before you build

Before writing a line, confirm demand: are people already searching for and paying to solve this? A $19 product that solves a recurring headache beats a $99 product nobody has heard of. Spend an hour on validation; it saves a week of building the wrong thing.

Ship to where demand lives

A platform like Gumroad (open-source, creator-friendly) puts your product in front of an existing buyer audience instead of forcing you to invent traffic from zero. List the v1, learn from the first buyers, then expand.

Why build-once beats grind

A digital product earns while you sleep only if it solves a real, recurring problem and is genuinely correct. The AI speeds the draft; your judgment makes it sellable. The playbook walks the full pipeline from idea to listed product.

The edit pass that makes it correct

AI drafts are fluent before they are right. After generating, read the asset as a buyer would: every number checked, every instruction runnable, nothing vague. A product that quietly lies loses the only asset you have — trust. The edit pass is not optional; it is the difference between a listing and a refund.
