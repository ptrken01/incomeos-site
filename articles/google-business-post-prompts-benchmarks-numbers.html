Key Optimization Techniques

1. Prompt Structure Refinement

The most significant speed improvement came from simplifying my prompt structure. Here's what I found:

Before (slow):

```python

prompt = f"""

You are a marketing expert for local businesses.

Create a Google Business post about: {topic}

Include:

  • A compelling headline
  • 2-3 bullet points
  • Call to action
  • Relevant hashtags
  • Emoji usage

"""

```

After (fast):

```python

prompt = f"""

Create a Google Business post about '{topic}'.

Use 100-150 words, include emoji, 2-3 key points, call to action.

Hashtags: #localbusiness #smallbusiness

"""

```

This simple change reduced my average response time from 28 seconds to 12 seconds.
