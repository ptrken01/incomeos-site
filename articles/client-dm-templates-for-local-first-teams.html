Client DM Templates for Local-First Teams

Building effective client relationships starts with consistent, personalized communication. For local fitness coaches managing multiple clients, this means crafting individualized direct messages that drive engagement without feeling spammy.

The Workflow Challenge

Most coaches spend 2-3 hours weekly on client communication. That's time better spent on training or program development. We've identified the root issue: most DM templates are generic and require manual customization for each client.

Here's a practical solution using structured templates that scale:

```python

Template structure for personalized client DMs

def generate_client_dm(client_name, program_status, recent_achievement):

dm_templates = {

"motivational": f"Hey {client_name}! 🔥 Your dedication to {program_status} is incredible. Just saw your {recent_achievement} - that's exactly the progress we're aiming for!",

"progress_check": f"Hi {client_name}, how are you feeling about {program_status}? I wanted to check in and see if you need any adjustments to your plan.",

"encouragement": f"Good morning {client_name}! Remember why you started this journey. You've got {recent_achievement} under your belt - let's keep building on that!"

}

return dm_templates.get("motivational", dm_templates["progress_check"])

Usage example

print(generate_client_dm("Sarah", "weight loss program", "5 lb weight loss"))

```

This approach reduces DM creation time from 5-10 minutes per client to under 2 minutes when using pre-built template variables. The key is having consistent, flexible templates that require minimal customization.
