The Key Metrics That Matter

Focus on these three core metrics when building your digital products:

2. Average Order Value: $20–$50 is typical for most digital products

3. Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR): Target $1,000+ for sustainable passive income

Your initial effort should focus on creating one product that generates at least $100/month, then scale from there.
