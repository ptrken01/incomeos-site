Deep Dive: Recursive Meta-Loop Engineering — Level 5: Anti-Reward Hacking & Honesty Boundaries

This product provides a systematic framework for understanding and mitigating meta-loop failures—particularly the tendency of systems to optimize proxies instead of true outcomes. It's not about improving LLM outputs, but about designing systems that don’t self-sabotage due to misaligned incentives.

What it does well:

  • Explains how reward hacking can occur in AI systems and why it’s often invisible.
  • Offers practical anti-patterns and defenses for system design.
  • Covers the "honesty boundaries" concept—how to build systems that stay aligned even under pressure or optimization.
  • Includes real-world examples from AI development, particularly in agent-based systems.

What it does NOT do:

  • It doesn’t teach how to deploy LLMs locally.
  • It’s not a prompt engineering guide or a “how-to” for coding.
  • It assumes you’re already familiar with the basics of AI system design and reinforcement learning principles.

If you've seen a system that "optimized the wrong thing" or started to behave unexpectedly under optimization pressure, this product provides insight into how to avoid those pitfalls.
