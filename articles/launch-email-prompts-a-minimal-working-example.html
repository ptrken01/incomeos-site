Launch Email Prompts: A Minimal Working Example

When building course launches, the email sequence often determines conversion success. The launch email is your first opportunity to convince prospects they need your course. Here's a minimal working example that you can immediately adapt.

```python

import os

from jinja2 import Template

Your launch email template

template_str = """

Subject: {{course_name}} - Your 7-Day Roadmap to {{desired_outcome}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I'm excited to share the {{course_name}} roadmap with you. This course is designed for busy professionals like you who want to {{desired_outcome}} without the overwhelm.

Here's what we'll cover:

1. {{topic_1}} - {{description_1}}

2. {{topic_2}} - {{description_2}}

3. {{topic_3}} - {{description_3}}

You'll walk away with:

  • A clear action plan
  • Practical frameworks
  • Step-by-step exercises

Ready to get started? Click here: {{signup_link}}

Best,

{{instructor_name}}

"""

Your data

email_data = {

"course_name": "Content Marketing Mastery",

"first_name": "Alex",

"desired_outcome": "build a sustainable content marketing strategy",

"topic_1": "Audience Research",

"description_1": "Identify your ideal audience and create buyer personas",

"topic_2": "Content Strategy",

"description_2": "Develop a content calendar that drives engagement",

"topic_3": "Conversion Optimization",

"description_3": "Turn content into measurable results",

"signup_link": "https://example.com/signup",

"instructor_name": "Sarah Johnson"

}

Render the email

template = Template(template_str)

email_content = template.render(**email_data)

print(email_content)

```

This minimal example demonstrates how to structure a launch email with variables that can be swapped out for different courses. The template approach allows you to build once, launch many.

The key elements of this launch email include:

  • A compelling subject line with the course name and benefit
  • Personalization with the recipient's first name
  • Clear outline of what will be covered
  • Value proposition of what attendees will achieve
  • Strong call-to-action with a signup link

You can expand this system by adding more variables for different course types, or by integrating with email marketing platforms like Mailchimp or ConvertKit. The template structure supports multiple variations while maintaining consistency in your messaging.

FAQ

Q: How do I customize the launch email for different course types?

A: Add more variables to your data dictionary and template. For example, include "course_duration", "target_audience", or "prerequisites" as needed. This allows you to maintain a core structure while adapting content specifics.

Q: What makes a launch email conversion rate better than average?

A: Strong personalization, clear value proposition, social proof, and urgency. The example above includes the benefit statement ("without the overwhelm") and specific outcomes that resonate with busy professionals.

Q: Can I use this template with email automation tools?

A: Yes. Most email platforms support templating systems that mirror this approach. You'd replace the Python rendering with your platform's variable system, but the core structure remains identical for consistent messaging across campaigns.
