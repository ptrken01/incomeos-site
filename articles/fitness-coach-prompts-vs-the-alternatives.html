Understanding the Prompt Landscape

Most coaches I've worked with use generic AI prompts that produce mediocre results. These often require extensive editing and don't account for client-specific variables. The key difference lies in specificity: effective prompts are built for a particular use case and incorporate your coaching methodology.

Here's a practical example of how to structure conversion-focused DM prompts:

```prompt

Create a 3-line direct message that addresses a client's concern about time constraints while highlighting a 20-minute high-intensity workout program that can be done at home. Include a call-to-action that encourages booking a consultation.

[Client Profile: 35-year-old marketing manager, 15 lbs to lose, works 60+ hours/week]

Your response should be formatted as:

  • Opening hook (1 line)

  • Call-to-action (1 line)

```

This approach produces consistent, actionable results that can be adapted for different client profiles.
