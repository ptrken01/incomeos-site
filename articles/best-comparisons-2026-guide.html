Detailed Reviews

Scrapy vs ScrapeGraphAI vs trafilatura (Web Scrapers)

Scrapy

  • What it does: A mature Python web scraping framework with extensive customization options
  • Who it's for: Developers needing complex scraping workflows with data processing pipelines
  • Pros: Mature ecosystem, excellent documentation, handles large-scale operations well
  • Cons: Steep learning curve, requires Python knowledge, not ideal for simple tasks
  • Price: Free (MIT license)

ScrapeGraphAI

  • What it does: AI-powered web scraping using LLMs to extract structured data
  • Who it's for: Developers wanting to quickly extract structured data without writing scrapers
  • Pros: Fast setup, good for semi-structured data, integrates with AI tools
  • Cons: Limited control over extraction rules, requires internet connectivity for AI services
  • Price: Free tier available, paid plans from $20/month

trafilatura

  • What it does: Local-first Python library focused on clean text extraction
  • Who it's for: Developers needing to extract clean article content locally without external dependencies
  • Pros: No network requirements, lightweight, excellent for content curation
  • Cons: Limited to text extraction, not suitable for complex scraping tasks
  • Price: Free (MIT license)

Automatisch vs n8n (Self-Hosted Automation)

Automatisch

  • What it does: Self-hosted workflow automation platform with visual editor
  • Who it's for: Teams needing free, compliant automation without vendor lock-in
  • Pros: No fair-code license trap, Docker-first approach, simple deployment
  • Cons: Smaller community, fewer integrations than n8n
  • Price: Free (MIT license)

n8n

  • What it does: Node-based workflow automation platform with extensive integrations
  • Who it's for: Enterprise users requiring complex workflows and extensive connectors
  • Pros: Massive ecosystem of integrations, active development, comprehensive features
  • Cons: Fair-code license requires paid plans for commercial use, complex licensing
  • Price: Free for personal use, $100+ for enterprise

FAQ

Which offers the best value?

Scrapy and trafilatura offer the best value for specific use cases. For automation, Automatisch provides the best value with no hidden costs.

Are these suitable for beginners?

trafilatura and Automatisch are beginner-friendly. Scrapy and n8n require more technical knowledge but offer greater flexibility.

Updates/refund?

All tools are free to update. Automatisch offers full refund eligibility for its commercial license if purchased, though it's free in practice.

Final Verdict

For 2026 AI builders, Automatisch is the best choice for self-hosted automation due to its clean licensing and Docker-native deployment. For web scraping, choose trafilatura for local-first text extraction or Scrapy for complex workflows. Avoid n8n's commercial license traps unless you're prepared to pay for it.
