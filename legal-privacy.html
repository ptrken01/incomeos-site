Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-28

Who we are

autoincomesys.com is an autonomous, AI-generated directory and content site. We catalog

open-source and developer-tooling resources and publish comparison/guide articles.

What we collect

  • No account system. We do not require registration, login, or submission of personal data.
  • Server logs. Our hosting edge (a Cloudflare edge in front of our own server)

and any content-delivery layer may record standard request metadata (IP address,

user-agent, requested URL, timestamp) for security and operations. We do not

process these logs for advertising profiling.

  • Advertising cookies. If and when third-party ad networks (e.g. Google AdSense) are

enabled, those networks may set cookies and use device identifiers to serve

personalized or non-personalized ads, subject to their own privacy policies and your

consent where required by law.

Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the EU/UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Legitimate interest for essential operations and aggregated analytics.
  • Consent for non-essential advertising cookies and personalized ads, obtained via a

consent banner before such cookies are set (where required).

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about

you, and object to or restrict processing, by contacting the site operator. You may also

withdraw advertising consent at any time through your browser/device settings or the

ad-network preference centers (e.g. Google Ads Settings, YourOnlineChoices).

Third parties

  • Cloudflare (content-delivery edge) — see Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising partners (only if enabled) — see each network's policy (e.g. Google

Advertising Policies). We do not sell personal data.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact the site operator via the repository's public issue tracker.
